Our Story

Built for Federal Work

WANZIKI LLC was founded by Frankline Nganje — a PMP, CISA, and ServiceNow CSA-certified program manager with over a decade of enterprise IT leadership in regulated, high-complexity environments.

The firm was built around a specific gap in the federal marketplace: organizations need program managers who don't just manage GRC programs — they need someone who understands GRC deeply. That rare combination of governance expertise and PM discipline is the core of everything WANZIKI LLC delivers.

Headquartered in Buford, Georgia (Atlanta metro), WANZIKI LLC is a Black-owned, minority-owned small business with an active SAM.gov registration, ready to engage on federal subcontracts, teaming agreements, and prime opportunities today.
